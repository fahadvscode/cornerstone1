## Pre-Construction vs. Resale: Why It Matters for Budget Buyers

Buyers targeting sub-$700K townhomes face a fork in the road: pre-construction or resale. Each path has distinct advantages.

### Pre-Construction Advantages

- **Lower entry pricing** before market appreciation during construction

- **Modern layouts and technology** (like Futura at Cornerstone Towns)

### Resale Advantages

- **Immediate occupancy** — no waiting for construction
- **Visible product** — you tour the actual home before buying
- **Negotiable pricing** in softer market conditions
- **Established neighbourhoods** with mature trees and amenities

For budget-conscious buyers in 2026, pre-construction often wins on total cost of ownership. A townhome purchased from the $600s pre-construction may appraise significantly higher by occupancy — building equity before you even move in.

## Understanding the Pre-Construction Deposit Structure

One of the biggest advantages of pre-construction for sub-$700K buyers is the deposit schedule. Instead of requiring a full down payment at purchase, builders spread deposits across the construction timeline.

Typical pre-construction deposit structures in the GTA:

- **5% on signing**
- **5% at 30–60 days**
- **5% at 90 days**
- **5% at 180 days**
- **5% at occupancy** (or earlier milestones)

That means a $650,000 townhome might require only $32,500 at signing — far more manageable than the $130,000+ needed for a 20% down payment on a resale purchase.

## First-Time Buyer Considerations

If you are a first-time buyer targeting a sub-$700K townhome, several programs and strategies can help:

The federal First-Time Home Buyer Incentive provides shared equity funding toward your down payment. Eligibility and amounts vary — consult a mortgage professional for current terms.
