## What Is the Futura Smart Home Package?

Futura is Primont Homes' integrated smart home technology suite, designed to be installed during construction rather than retrofitted after move-in. That distinction matters: hardwired systems are more reliable, more seamless, and more valuable at resale than aftermarket gadgets stuck to walls with adhesive strips.

At Cornerstone Towns, Futura is included in every unit regardless of floor plan — whether you choose a one-bedroom-plus-den, a three-bedroom family layout, a back-to-back town, or a traditional townhome with rear yard access.

## Futura Features: Complete Breakdown

### Smart Thermostat

The Futura package includes a **smart thermostat** that learns your household's heating and cooling patterns and adjusts automatically to reduce energy consumption.

Why this matters:

- **Lower utility bills** through intelligent scheduling and occupancy detection
- **Remote control** via smartphone app — adjust temperature before you arrive home
- **Energy efficiency** aligns with Ontario's push toward greener housing
- **Resale appeal** — smart thermostats are among the most requested features in modern home listings

For a typical Brampton townhome, intelligent climate control can save hundreds of dollars annually on gas and electricity, compounding over the life of your ownership.

### Camera Doorbell

Security and convenience converge with the **camera doorbell**, which lets you see and speak to visitors from anywhere using your smartphone.

Key benefits:

- **Package delivery monitoring** — know exactly when deliveries arrive and deter porch pirates
- **Visitor screening** — verify who is at your door before opening it
- **Remote communication** — speak to delivery drivers, guests, or service providers when you are not home
- **Recorded footage** provides peace of mind for families with children or elderly residents

In a community of 129 townhomes where neighbours are still getting acquainted, a camera doorbell adds a layer of security that residents consistently rank among the most valued smart home features.

### Motion-Activated Front Door Light

The **motion-activated front door light** enhances safety and visibility when you arrive home after dark. It eliminates fumbling for keys in poorly lit entryways and deters unwanted visitors by illuminating the entrance automatically.

This feature is simple but impactful — particularly for families with young children, shift workers arriving home late, and elderly residents who prioritize well-lit pathways.

### USB and USB-C Charging Outlets

Every home includes **USB and USB-C charging outlets** built into the kitchen and every bedroom. No more hunting for adapters or occupying standard outlets with phone chargers.

Why built-in charging matters in 2026:

- **Device proliferation** — households routinely charge phones, tablets, smartwatches, earbuds, and laptops simultaneously
- **Clean aesthetics** — no cable clutter around kitchen counters and bedside tables
- **Future-proofing** — USB-C is the emerging standard for modern electronics
- **Convenience** — charge devices where you actually use them

For remote workers who set up home offices in dens or bedrooms, built-in charging at the point of use is a daily quality-of-life improvement.

### EV Charging-Ready Garage

Electric vehicle adoption in the GTA is accelerating. Ontario's EV incentive programs, expanding charging infrastructure, and rising fuel costs are pushing more buyers toward electric and hybrid vehicles.

The Futura package includes an **EV charging-ready garage** — pre-wired with conduit for a future electric vehicle charger. This means:

- **No expensive retrofit** when you purchase an EV down the road
- **Faster installation** of a Level 2 charger when you are ready
- **Resale advantage** as EV-ready homes command premiums in an increasingly electrified market
- **Future compliance** with potential building code requirements for EV infrastructure

Installing EV charging infrastructure during construction costs a fraction of what retrofits demand after drywall is sealed and garages are finished.
