## What Is Cornerstone Towns?

Cornerstone Towns is Phase 1 of a much larger master-planned community that will eventually welcome **18,000+ residents at full buildout**. The first release consists of **129 freehold townhomes**, offering a range of layouts from compact one-bedroom-plus-den units to spacious three-bedroom family homes.

Unlike leasehold or condo-townhouse products, these are **freehold townhomes**, meaning you own the land your home sits on. That distinction matters for long-term equity growth, rental potential, and the freedom to renovate without condo board restrictions.

Primont Homes has positioned Cornerstone Towns as a community built for modern living — smart technology included as standard, efficient layouts, and a location that balances suburban comfort with genuine transit connectivity.

## Why Freehold Matters in 2026

The GTA townhome market includes a mix of freehold and condo-townhouse products, and the distinction affects your ownership experience and long-term returns.

**Freehold townhomes** at Cornerstone mean:

- You own the land, not just the unit
- No monthly condo maintenance fees on the structure (standard property taxes apply)
- Greater renovation freedom
- Stronger resale appeal in many Brampton submarkets
- Better rental income potential without condo restrictions
